# PM Reps Portfolio Handoff

## One-line pitch

PM Reps turns verified AI product launches into short judgment exercises that preserve the learner's original decision, evidence-linked feedback, revision, and recruiter-readable proof.

## Why this project fits the builder

The project combines three parts of the intended AI product-manager profile:

- statistical discipline: no invented baselines, bounded rubrics, explicit unavailable evidence, and evaluation gates;
- product judgment: user, evidence, metric, AI-system, and rollout decisions rather than generic chat;
- product communication: a private learning loop becomes an optional public Decision Card without exposing private history.

## Interview story

### Problem

Reading AI product news creates recognition but not repeated decision practice. Existing interview simulators often reward framework language or agreement with a hidden answer.

### Product decision

Require commitment before revealing the real launch. Evaluate reasoning across five dimensions, keep alternatives valid, and make revision a completion requirement.

### Trust decision

Build separate pre-commit, reveal, private-attempt, evaluation, and public-card projections. Never hide sensitive fields with client-side CSS. Failed model output cannot create progress or public content.

### Measurement decision

Use completed and revised reps per active week as the north-star behavior. Do not invent target values before dogfooding creates a baseline.

### AI decision

Keep a transparent rules evaluator so the product remains usable without a model credential. Add a structured-output model adapter behind schema and evidence validation, and label the active mode in the interface.

### Source decision

Use AI HOT only as a current candidate inbox. Treat summaries as untrusted, preserve attribution, and require human source verification before activation.

## Suggested demo sequence

1. Open Today's Rep and show that company and outcome are absent.
2. Complete four decisions and commit.
3. Reveal the real launch and official source.
4. Generate five-dimension evidence feedback.
5. Revise one rationale and complete the rep.
6. Show the completed-only Skill Map update.
7. Preview, publish, open, and unpublish the Decision Card.
8. Open Case Inbox to explain the human review gate for current AI product launches.

## Honest boundaries to state

- The private release has one active manually verified case.
- The current deployed evaluator uses the labeled rules baseline unless a model credential is configured.
- Model consistency and bilingual quality are not yet claimed because those baselines are unavailable.
- The next product milestone is evidence collection, not adding more dashboard features.
